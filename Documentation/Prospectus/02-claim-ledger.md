# Claim Ledger - FTSArcade / Boundless San Francisco

**Version:** S0 · **Date:** 2026-10-14
**Rules:** No prospectus sentence may introduce a claim absent from this ledger. Four claim types only: **Factual** (observable/documented), **Operational** (what the project will do), **Causal** (why an activity should produce a result), **Forward-looking** (what is expected to continue/change later). Evidence grades: **E1** direct, **E2** closely comparable, **E3** analogous, **E4** plausible untested assumption (must include a test), **E5** insufficient (remove). A claim may move down in confidence, never up. Owners are roles - **named persons are required** (`[INFORMATION NEEDED: ...]`). Claim count: **24** (within the 15-25 band).

## Prohibited-claims quick list (drawn from C19-C24 and the methodology blacklist)

Do not use in any draft: "revolutionary," "world-class," "unique," "cutting-edge"; "empower," "unlock," "transform" without a defined change; "scalable" without unit cost and constraint; "community-led" without a defined community authority; "sustainable" without a payer/operating model; "partners include" when discussions are incomplete; "data shows" when the source is an assumption; "at scale" without a number; "and more" after a list; "solutions for San Francisco" without naming population and condition.

## Evidence-register linkage

Each causal/forward-looking claim above needs a row in the evidence register (`03-registers.md` §3) with source, population/context, finding, limitation, grade, and test plan. C19 and C20 are the only causal claims currently graded E4 with tests; C21 and C22 are removed; C12-C18 are operational/forward-looking with tests to be defined by their owners.
