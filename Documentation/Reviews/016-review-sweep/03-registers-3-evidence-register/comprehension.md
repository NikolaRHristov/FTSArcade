## Restatement

Section 3 is the evidence register: a table of claim rows (Claim ID · Evidence · Source · Population/context · Finding · Limitation · Grade · Test plan) recording what the packet asserts, on what basis, at what certainty, and what must happen next. Status is S0: only the E1 rows (site retrieval C01-C04; rules/FAQ C06) are complete, while the E4 rows need test plans owned by the evidence reviewer once the program is defined. The rows span verified product and rules facts, untested charitable-frame hypotheses (C19-C20), asserted-but-undocumented team capabilities (C12-C18), claims prohibited as E5 (C21-C22), and documented absences graded E1-as-absence / E5-as-positive-claim (C07, C08, C23, C24). The register's function is to separate facts from hypotheses from prohibitions, and to tie every row to a verification step.
